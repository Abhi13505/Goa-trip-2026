South Goa in motion

Rules for the Crew

A few things to keep everyone alive and thriving.

Transportation

Rent scooters for the North to beat traffic. Use cabs for the long transition to the South.

Hydration Protocol

Goa is humid and the nights are long. Force everyone to drink tender coconut water every morning.

Go with the Flow

It's a vacation. If the crew sleeps in, you sleep in. If you want to sit at a shack all day, do it. Vibe over schedule.

Goa '26

Sun, sand, and unforgettable memories. Pack light, stay hydrated, and go with the flow.
