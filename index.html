South Goa in motion

Rules for the Crew

A few essentials to keep the entire group thriving.

Transportation

Rent scooters for the North to beat narrow street traffic. Use pre-booked cabs for the long transition to the South.

Hydration Protocol

Goa is hot and humid. Pick up tender coconut water and keep mineral water bottles handy on the scooters.

Go with the Flow

It's a vacation. If the crew sleeps in, you sleep in. If you want to sit at a shack all day, do it. Vibe over schedule.

Goa '26

Sun, sand, and unforgettable memories. Pack light, stay hydrated, and go with the flow.
